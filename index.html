Kartik Amavasya · The darkest night

दीपावली

The festival of lights — the night an entire kingdom lit the way home for its king.

Chapter I · Fourteen years

The Long Exile

Bound by his father’s promise, Prince Ram left Ayodhya for fourteen years in the forest, with devoted Sita and brother Lakshman. Ayodhya waited in shadow — and Bharata ruled in Ram’s name, with his sandals upon the throne.

Chapter II · Lanka

Dharma Prevails

Ravana abducted Sita. With Hanuman and the vanara army, Ram crossed the sea and defeated the demon king of Lanka. Light had conquered darkness; justice, injustice.

Chapter III · The Return

The Pushpak Vimana

Aboard the celestial flying chariot Pushpak, Ram, Sita and Lakshman soared north across the sky toward home, as the exile ended on a moonless night.

Chapter IV · Ayodhya awakens

A Million Diyas

The people lit rows of earthen lamps along every street, rooftop and the banks of the Sarayu, so that no shadow could hide the road. Joy, music and sweets filled the city. Citizens thronged the streets, showering petals and cheering as Ram, Sita, Lakshman and Hanuman walked home. Ram was crowned — and Ram Rajya began.
